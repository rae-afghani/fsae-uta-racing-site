About UTA Racing

UTA Racing is The University of Texas at Arlington’s Formula SAE team. Students design, build, test, and race formula-style cars.

Student-built since 1982

UTA Racing gives students hands-on experience across design, fabrication, testing, and competition.

Texas Autocross

UTA hosts student teams and alumni in Arlington for a weekend on the course, bringing together current builds and cars from earlier seasons.
